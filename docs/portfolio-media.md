# TalkRounds: screenshots and demo recording

Use only made-up names. The fake students (`npm run fake-students -- <code>`) are called Alex, Bea,
Carlos, Dee…; type **Sam** as the name on your own phone. Hide the browser's bookmarks bar, close
other tabs, and turn on "Do not disturb" on both devices.

## Setup for both screenshots and the recording

1. Teacher device (laptop or iPad, landscape): open the landing page and tap **Try it now with your
   class**.
2. Setup: level **B1**, theme **Travel & Nature**, **1 min** rounds, **Take turns**, timer
   **Automatic**. Tap **Create session**.
3. On a computer: `npm run fake-students -- <the 6-digit code> 3` (3 fake students + your phone = two
   pairs).
4. Phone: scan the QR code, type **Sam**, tap **Join**.

## 60–90 second recording script (two devices)

Record the laptop screen and the phone screen at the same time (or put the phone next to the laptop
and film both), then edit them side by side. Times are approximate. The two waits for the timer
(rounds are 1 minute) are trimmed in editing.

| Time | Laptop (teacher) | Phone (student) | Voice-over |
|------|------------------|-----------------|------------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Landing page, slow scroll | n/a | "TalkRounds turns speaking practice into speed friending: every student talks to everyone." |
| 0:08–0:18 | Tap **Try it now**, pick B1, Travel, Take turns, **Create session** | n/a | "Pick a level and a theme. No account and no app needed." |
| 0:18–0:28 | QR code; names appear as students join | Scan the QR code, type **Sam**, **Join** | "Students scan the code and type their name." |
| 0:28–0:40 | **Start session**: pairs show "waiting", then turn green | "Find your partner: Carlos", tap **We found each other!** | "First they find their partner. The round starts when every pair has checked in." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Timer: "First half" | "You ask · Carlos talks", question and follow-up prompts | "One student asks, with follow-up prompts to keep their partner talking…" |
| 0:55–1:05 | *(trim the wait)* Timer reaches half time | **Swap!**, then "Your turn to talk" | "…then they swap." |
| 1:05–1:15 | *(trim the wait)* Round 2: new pairs | "Find your partner: Alex" | "When time's up, everyone moves to a new partner." |
| 1:15–1:25 | **End session** | "Session complete" | "Try the demo on the website, or run it with your class." |

Afterwards, stop the fake students with Ctrl+C. The session and its names are deleted automatically
after 30 days.
